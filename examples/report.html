How the ranking works

  1. Clean. Note prefixes are stripped, and the same customer posting the same thing twice is counted once. Sentences that only express urgency (“please prioritize this”) are kept for scoring but removed from theme detection.
  2. Find themes. TF-IDF on stemmed words and phrases, compressed with LSA, clustered with k-means (k = 12 chosen by silhouette score); split themes that share defining words are merged back together. Clear praise is routed to “What's working”.
  3. Reach = accounts raising the theme, weighted by plan (Free 1, Standard 2, Premium 3, Enterprise 5).
  4. Impact (RICE scale 0.25–3) from pain (VADER sentiment) and urgency language: blocked work, churn risk, willingness to pay.
  5. Confidence (50/80/100%) from how many channels and plans corroborate it, and volume.
  6. Opportunity score = Reach × Impact × Confidence. With engineering estimates (--review), the RICE score divides by effort in person-weeks, which is where the engineering view comes in.

Scores rank themes against each other within this dataset; they are a starting point for discovery, not a substitute for talking to customers.
